Daily Brief

Daily Brief is a personal tool. Each morning it reads the owner's own Gmail and Google Workspace mailboxes, read-only, and writes a single summary: what needs a reply, what is due, what can wait.

What it does

  • Reads recent messages from the owner's own mailboxes using the read-only Gmail permission.
  • Ranks messages and writes short summaries of them.
  • Lets the owner approve follow-ups and keep track of them.

What it never does

  • It never sends, replies to, archives, labels or deletes any email.
  • It is not offered to the public. Only the owner signs in, and only to their own mailboxes.
  • It does not sell data, show ads, or share mail content with anyone for their own use.

How data is handled, including every service involved, is described in the privacy policy.
